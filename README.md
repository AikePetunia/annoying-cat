# Annoying-cat !

*When your cat approaches your desktop and you can't see what's going on...*

**Annoying Cat** is a browser extension designed to cure digital addiction and break the infinite scrolling loop. Set strict daily or hourly limits for posts, tweets, and shorts, filter your feeds, and cut through the noise on social media. 

**[Get it for Firefox](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/annoying-cat/)**

## Features:

- **Everywhere:** block Twitter/X, Instagram and Reddit completely, grayscale mode, hide anything that mentions your keywords.
- **Twitter/X:** hourly/daily tweet limit, "Following" tab only, hide metrics and clutter.
- **YouTube:** block shorts or limit them, hide comments and metrics.
- **Instagram:** limit or remove posts and stories, block reels, hide comments, metrics and suggestions.
